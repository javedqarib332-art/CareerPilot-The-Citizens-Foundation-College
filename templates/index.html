Before You Start

How This Works

This isn't a test you can pass or fail — there are no right or wrong answers. Just answer honestly based on what genuinely feels true for you. It takes about 15-20 minutes and has 4 short sections.

Section 1 — Interests (32 questions)

You'll see short everyday scenarios and rate how much you'd enjoy doing each one:

  • Strongly dislike — this sounds unpleasant or draining to you
  • Dislike — you'd rather not do this
  • Neutral — you don't feel strongly either way
  • Enjoy — this sounds like something you'd genuinely like doing
  • Strongly enjoy — this really excites you, you'd jump at the chance

Section 2 — Personality (20 questions)

You'll see short statements about how you generally are, and say how much each one sounds like you:

  • Strongly disagree — this really isn't like you at all
  • Disagree — this isn't really how you are
  • Neutral — sometimes true, sometimes not, depends on the day
  • Agree — this is generally how you are
  • Strongly agree — this is exactly how you are, almost always

Section 3 — Skills (8 questions)

For each skill, rate how confident you feel from 1 (not confident at all) to 5 (very confident), and briefly say why in one line. Be honest here — this isn't about impressing anyone, it helps us understand you better.

Section 4 — Academic Performance (6 subjects)

Rate how you actually do in each subject, from 1 (weak — this is genuinely hard for you) to 5 (strong — you consistently do well). This is about your real performance, not just whether you enjoy the subject.
